**Context:** Edward Stratemeyer (1862–1930) ran a fiction factory — outlines to ghostwriters, pseudonyms, series branding — that produced * Nancy Drew*, *The Hardy Boys*, *Tom Swift*, *The Bobbsey Twins*, and many more. These NYPL items are internal business records, not the published novels themselves.
